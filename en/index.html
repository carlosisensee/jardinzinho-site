A studio with a point of view

Playful by nature.
Precise by craft.

Jardinzinho sits between fashion, textile memory and everyday experimentation. The result is not a conventional collection, but a growing body of garments shaped slowly in a small Berlin studio.

“The starting point is often something that already exists.”

A forgotten fabric, a leftover print, a garment that deserves another chance. Each piece begins with what is available and grows through composition, colour and sewing.

The atelier

Where the pieces take shape.

A small, sunlit working room in Berlin — full of books, notes, tools and personal objects. This is where fabrics are tested, combined, cut and slowly turned into one-of-one garments.

made here, slowly ✿
Selected pieces

Florals, stripes, nostalgic prints and contrasting panels are brought together without trying to make them quiet. The dresses are romantic, but never timid.

Process

From found textile to finished piece.

The process is deliberately hands-on. Materials are chosen for their character first, then recomposed around a new silhouette.

01

Collect

Existing garments, remnants, vintage fabrics and overlooked prints become the raw material.

02

Compose

Colours and motifs are placed next to each other until a new visual rhythm begins to appear.

03

Cut & stitch

The final garment is built manually in the Berlin atelier, with details adjusted piece by piece.

04

Wear again

A material that already had a history leaves the studio with a different shape and another life.

Material philosophy

Nothing has to match to belong together.

The visual identity comes from combination rather than uniformity. The atelier works with what is available, letting different fabrics keep their own character inside a new garment.

ReclaimedFabrics and garments that can be used again.
Small scaleNo mass production. Pieces are made individually or in very small runs.
ExpressiveStrong prints, flowers and colour are treated as a signature, not decoration.
