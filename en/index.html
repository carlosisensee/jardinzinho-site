A studio with a point of view

Playful by nature.
Precise by craft.

Jardinzinho sits between fashion, textile memory and everyday experimentation. The result is not a conventional collection, but a growing body of one-of-one garments shaped slowly in a small Berlin studio.

“The starting point is often something that already exists.”

A leftover textile, a forgotten print or a material that deserves another chance. The value is in combination, improvisation and careful making rather than uniformity.

Inside the atelier

A real place for colour, material and making.

The room is part of the story. Fabrics are gathered here, combinations are tested, patterns are altered and the garments are actually sewn.

Selected works

No series.
Just individual stories.

Every piece is created once, or in a very limited variation. Current work and available dresses are shown through Instagram and TikTok.

Atelier process

From form to garment

The process

Made slowly.
Decided personally.

The aim is not to produce more, but to create something distinctive from what is already available.

01

Find

Existing textiles, remnants and found materials become the starting point.

02

Combine

Colours and prints are tested against one another until a new visual language appears.

03

Rebuild

Cut, proportion and details are adjusted for every individual piece.

04

Share directly

New work appears in the feed. Interest in a piece is handled personally by direct message.

Materials

What already exists can become something new.

Reclaimed textiles are not a neutral sustainability claim here. They are part of the design language, carrying their own colour, texture and past into the next piece.

reuse it. remix it. wear it.
FAQ

How do I get a piece?

Because the garments are unique, buying works differently from a conventional online shop.

Where can I see currently available pieces?

New work and available one-of-one garments are mainly shared on Instagram and TikTok.

How do I buy or reserve a dress?

Message Jardinzinho directly through Instagram or TikTok to ask about availability, measurements and additional photos.

Is every piece really unique?

The combinations depend on limited reclaimed textiles and remade materials, so many garments genuinely exist only once.
