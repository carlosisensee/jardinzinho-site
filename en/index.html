Clothes with a past. Reimagined in Berlin.

Jardinzinho is a small Berlin atelier run by a Japanese seamstress. Vintage fabrics, existing garments and textile finds become playful one-of-one pieces — colorful, floral and intentionally personal.

The idea

A small atelier for clothes you want to get to know.

Jardinzinho brings together Japanese craft, vintage aesthetics and Berlin remake culture. Not as an anonymous webshop, but as personal work where fabric, story and handcraft stay visible.

“A dress can feel like a little garden.”
庭園のように — like a garden

Florals, unexpected fabric combinations and visible handwork make every piece distinct.

Inside the atelier

This is where the pieces come to life.

The atelier is part of the work: a bright, personal space filled with fabrics, books, sketches and the sewing machine. Design, selection, cutting and making happen here in small, focused steps.

There is no industrial rhythm here. Each combination grows out of what is available — and what fabric, color and shape can become together.

From the feed

Work to discover — not add to a cart.

Because many pieces truly are one of one, there is no classic shopping cart. New work and currently available garments are shared first on Instagram and TikTok. If something feels right, the conversation starts with a message.

From found fabric to dress

Find. Reimagine. Stitch.

The character of the material has a say in the design. That is why a Jardinzinho piece rarely starts from a completely fixed template.

01

Find

Vintage fabrics, remnants and existing textiles are selected for pattern, quality and potential.

02

Reimagine

Colors and motifs are paired, shapes are changed and materials are moved beyond their original context.

03

Stitch

The piece is built, adjusted and finished by hand in the Berlin atelier.

How it works

Seen a piece you love?

How do I buy a Jardinzinho piece?

Simply send a direct message on Instagram or TikTok. You can ask whether the piece is still available, get its measurements and arrange the next steps personally.

Are the dresses one of a kind?

Many pieces are one-of-one. Vintage fabrics and remnants are limited, so combinations usually cannot be reproduced exactly.

Can I get more details before deciding?

Yes. Extra photos, measurements, material information and fit notes can be shared directly by message.

Where are the pieces made?

Design and handcrafting happen in the Berlin atelier.
