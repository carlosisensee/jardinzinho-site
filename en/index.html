Handmade · Remake · Berlin

Clothes with a past. Remade in Berlin.

Jardinzinho is a small independent atelier run by a Japanese seamstress in Berlin. Vintage fabrics, existing garments and textile finds are transformed into playful, colorful one-of-one pieces filled with florals, contrasts and a new story.

The idea

What already exists can become something singular.

Not every fabric needs a beginning as new raw material. Sometimes it simply needs a new point of view. Jardinzinho brings together Japanese craft, vintage aesthetics and Berlin remake culture.

“A dress can feel like a little garden.”
庭園のように — like a garden

Florals, unexpected fabric combinations and visible handwork make every piece distinct.

Inside the atelier

Small runs. A lot of handwork.

Design, fabric selection, cutting and sewing happen in Berlin. Materials are paired by color, pattern and character rather than by an industrial production plan.

Each dress is made as a one-of-one piece or in a very small run. Patterns and material combinations cannot be reproduced identically.

Selected pieces

Color that stays with you.

Jardinzinho works with bold florals, retro textiles, patchwork and contrasting details. The result is deliberately playful, vivid and personal.

The process

Find. Reimagine. Stitch.

The material has a voice in the design. That is why a Jardinzinho piece rarely starts from a completely fixed template.

01

Find

Vintage fabrics, remnants and existing textiles are selected for pattern, quality and potential.

02

Reimagine

Colors and motifs are paired, shapes are changed and materials are taken out of their original context.

03

Stitch

Pieces are built, adjusted and finished by hand in the Berlin atelier.

FAQ

Jardinzinho in a nutshell.

What is Jardinzinho?

Jardinzinho is an independent remake and handmade atelier in Berlin run by a Japanese seamstress, focused on colorful one-of-one garments made with vintage and reused textiles.

Are the dresses one of a kind?

Many pieces are one-of-one. Vintage fabrics and remnants are limited, so pattern combinations usually cannot be repeated exactly.

Where are the pieces made?

Design and handcrafting take place in Berlin.

Where can I see current pieces?

New work is shared on Instagram and in the Jardinzinho online shop.
